# Customer Churn Strategy

## Churn Prediction

The machine learning model generates a churn probability.

The churn probability should come from the trained machine learning
model and should not be invented by the language model.

## High Risk Customers

Customers with elevated churn risk may require proactive review.

## Supporting Factors

Repeated support issues, reduced usage, and contract renewal timing
may be considered when reviewing customer churn risk.

These factors are supporting business information and do not replace
the machine learning prediction.
