# Refund Policy

## Refund Eligibility

Customers can request a refund for eligible orders.

Refund eligibility depends on the order status, product condition,
and applicable refund rules.

## Refund Review

Refund requests should be reviewed against the applicable policy.

The AI assistant should not approve refunds automatically.

## Escalation

If the available information is insufficient, the request should
be escalated to the appropriate support team.
